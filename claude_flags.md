Issue 15 — Deployment cost vs. ACV/payback/gross margin needs an answer
Where: Sections 7 and 9 + Exhibit A
This remains the biggest internal financial question I see.
You say:
    •    Basic ACV = $62,500
    •    CAC = $25,000
    •    Payback = ~12 months
    •    Gross margin = 80%
    •    Deployment cost = $500,000 first-of-stack → $175,000 with connector reuse
Those numbers don't explain themselves.
If Grid Guardian actually incurs $175K of deployment cost for a customer paying $62.5K/year, the stated gross margin/payback doesn't appear to work.
There may be a completely legitimate explanation—for example, $500K/$175K could represent reusable platform-development/integration costs rather than per-customer COGS, or customers/design partners may cost-share it. But the paper currently calls it “deployment cost per customer” in Exhibit A.
What to do: Ask whoever built the financial model:
“Is the $500K/$175K deployment cost actually incurred per customer, and is it included in the 80% gross margin and CAC/payback calculations? If not, what exactly does that number represent?”
Do not rewrite until you have their answer.
Priority: HIGH. I expect someone looking closely at the financials could ask this.
